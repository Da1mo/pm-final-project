# StreamLine Spotlight · Initiative Roadmap

**Team:** 2 engineers + 1 designer · **Sprint length:** 3 weeks

**Persona:** The Wanderer
**Primary metric:** Wanderer watch-through rate
**Guardrail:** Power User sessions / week ≥ 4.5 (now 4.8)

Value and effort are scored 1–5 against the Wanderer's moment of misery: twenty minutes of scrolling, nothing found, the user leaves.

## NOW: current 3-week sprint

| # | Feature | Value | Effort | Quadrant | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | Spotlight Curated Rail | 5 | 2 | Quick Win | Directly replaces the twenty-minute scroll with a short, trusted set. It's the core of the initiative, and it's already live (Dec cohort), so this is refinement, not a build. |
| A2 | 'Why You'll Love This' Label | 4 | 2 | Quick Win | Tells the user why a title is worth it, so the pick feels personal. Make it a curator-written line, not AI, to match the strategy, and use tap instead of hover so it works on mobile and TV. |
| A6 | Spotlight Digest Email | 4 | 2 | Quick Win | Wanderers open the app only about 1.1 times a week, and this brings them back with a decision already made. It's the model of the competitor Tom left for (UXR-04). Send curated picks, not algorithmic ones. |

## Cut list

Deliberately not building for this initiative.

| # | Feature | Value | Effort | Quadrant | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A4 | Mood-Based Entry Point | 4 | 3 | Major Project | Matches the research directly ("quiet Sunday", UXR-09). But tagging the catalogue by mood is real editorial work, and a mood gate on login would add friction for Power Users and risk the guardrail. |
| A5 | Personalized Spotlight Queue | 2 | 5 | Time Sinker | Personalisation needs viewing history, and Wanderers barely have any. It's also algorithmic curation, which is the thing users distrust. |
| A7 | Curator Profiles | 2 | 3 | Time Sinker | Following people asks for commitment that infrequent users won't give. It's a Power User feature. |
| A8 | Watch Party (Spotlight) | 1 | 5 | Time Sinker | Nothing in the research asks for it, and it's a heavy real-time build that does nothing for the friction. |
| A9 | Advanced Filter Engine | 1 | 4 | Time Sinker | It gives users more choices to make, which makes the moment of misery worse. Wanderers don't filter. |
| A10 | Offline Download (Spotlight) | 1 | 4 | Time Sinker | Nothing in the research supports it, and offline viewing doesn't help a user decide what to watch. |

## Quadrant key

- **Quick Win:** high value, low effort
- **Major Project:** high value, high effort
- **Fill-In:** low value, low effort
- **Time Sinker:** low value, high effort
