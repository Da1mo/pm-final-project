# Feature Roadmap, Module 4 · StreamLine Spotlight

**Team:** 2 engineers + 1 designer

## Strategic anchors
- **Persona:** The Wanderer
- **Primary metric:** the Wanderer watch-through rate
- **Moment of misery:** After twenty minutes of scrolling through 15,000 titles, the user still hasn't found anything worth watching, so they give up and leave the app.
- **Guardrail:** Power User sessions per week, currently 4.8, must not fall below 4.5.

## Roadmap
### NOW, 3-week sprint
- **A1 Spotlight Curated Rail**, Directly replaces the twenty-minute scroll with a short, trusted set. It's the core of the initiative, and it's already live (Dec cohort), so this is refinement, not a build.
- **A2 'Why You'll Love This' Label**, Tells the user why a title is worth it, so the pick feels personal. Make it a curator-written line, not AI, to match the strategy, and use tap instead of hover so it works on mobile and TV.
- **A6 Spotlight Digest Email**, Wanderers open the app only about 1.1 times a week, and this brings them back with a decision already made. It's the model of the competitor Tom left for (UXR-04). Send curated picks, not algorithmic ones.

### NEXT, following 1-2 sprints
- _(none)_

### LATER, backlog
- **A3 Hidden Gem Badge**, Cheap, but it appeals to film buffs (Power Users, Marcus), not to Wanderers, who watch 61% Trending.

### ✂ Cut List
- **A4 Mood-Based Entry Point**, Matches the research directly ("quiet Sunday", UXR-09). But tagging the catalogue by mood is real editorial work, and a mood gate on login would add friction for Power Users and risk the guardrail.
- **A5 Personalized Spotlight Queue**, Personalisation needs viewing history, and Wanderers barely have any. It's also algorithmic curation, which is the thing users distrust.
- **A7 Curator Profiles**, Following people asks for commitment that infrequent users won't give. It's a Power User feature.
- **A8 Watch Party (Spotlight)**, Nothing in the research asks for it, and it's a heavy real-time build that does nothing for the friction.
- **A9 Advanced Filter Engine**, It gives users more choices to make, which makes the moment of misery worse. Wanderers don't filter.
- **A10 Offline Download (Spotlight)**, Nothing in the research supports it, and offline viewing doesn't help a user decide what to watch.
