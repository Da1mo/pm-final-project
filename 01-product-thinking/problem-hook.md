# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** StreamLine Spotlight
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If the company does nothing for 12 months will lose competitive advantage overtime. With the increase churn and declining engagement there will be less income.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** search for content on competitive platforms, or go search on communities for reviews of great content
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We must solve the growing subscriber churn and declining engagement by addressing users' frustration with an overwhelming content library that makes discovering high-quality, relevant films difficult, pushing them towards competing platforms and external discovery tools
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** We will initially invest in human curation and editorial collections, using StreamLine's existing catalogue to minimize content acquisition costs, while introducing a premium subscription for exclusive Spotlight access and postponing community suggestions and subscription rewards to a future phase to avoid additional development and operational expenses. We need to launch now to reverse rising churn, recover user engagement and protect our competitive position.
